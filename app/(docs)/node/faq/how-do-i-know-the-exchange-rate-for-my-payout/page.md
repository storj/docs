Payouts are made in USDC, a US dollar stablecoin, at one USDC per US dollar earned. There is no exchange rate to look up for USDC payouts: the amount of USDC you receive is the USD amount you earned.

For payouts you received in STORJ tokens before the switch to USDC, you can check the amount of USD a particular payment transaction was denominated in with our cost basis tool. You’ll need to enter your payment transaction:

[https://costbasis.storj.tools/](https://costbasis.storj.tools/)

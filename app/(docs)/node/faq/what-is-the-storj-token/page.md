STORJ is a utility token on the Ethereum network which follows the [ERC20 standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ERC20). It was previously used to pay Storage Node Operators and to pay for storage on the Storj network.

Storj now uses USD Coin (USDC) instead of STORJ. USDC is a stablecoin issued by a third party that is designed to hold a value of one US dollar. It is also an ERC20 token on the Ethereum network.

- **Storage Node Operators** receive their payouts in USDC on Ethereum (Layer 1). See [](docId:DVKqtMtnBdZ99gFRWCojP) for details.
- **Customers** can pay for storage by depositing USDC on Ethereum (Layer 1). See [payment methods](docId:7U4_uu6Pzg6u2N6FpV9VE) for details.

The zkSync Era Layer 2 payout method was [sunset on September 1, 2026](https://forum.storj.io/t/sunsetting-zksync-for-storagenode-payouts/32513).

You can use USD Coin (USDC) to buy storage on the Storj network and to pay for other services offered by the Storj platform, such as DNS and TLS automation.

{% callout type="info" %}
Storj no longer accepts the STORJ token as a form of payment, and the 10% bonus for paying with STORJ tokens has been discontinued.
{% /callout %}

The billing page for your account offers an option to select your payment method. This is where you would choose "Add USDC", which will then show the screen with your USDC deposit address and QR code. It accepts only USDC via L1 (Ethereum). Each USDC is credited to your account as one US dollar.

Once your USDC deposit is confirmed, its value will be shown in your Storj account on the billing page as available balance after an additional waiting period which may last up to several hours.

You can see more detailed instructions on how to pay with USDC in the [billing & payment](docId:Hurx0SirlRp_O5aUzew7_) section of our Storj documentation.
